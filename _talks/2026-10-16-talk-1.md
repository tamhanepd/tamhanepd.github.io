I was invited to give a CSPAR colloquium. I gave a colloquium titled ``Understanding AGN feedback in galaxy groups and clusters''. In this talk I gave a brief overview of galaxy cluster physics from observational point of view, introduced the cooling flow problem in galaxy clusters and presented my recent research on the residual cooling, filament formation and its stability and star formation in nearby groups and clusters.
