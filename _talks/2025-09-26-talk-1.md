I had an opportunity to talk to Frank Timmins from the American Astronomical Society's Journal Author Series about my paper on "Constraining star formation in M87 using Deep HST UV data," in a journal club–style interview, now available on YouTube.
It was a great chance to dive into the motivation, methods, and implications of our work on AGN feedback and star formation in M87, and to talk about how it fits into the broader picture of galaxy evolution research.
Many thanks to Frank and the AAS Journals team for organizing and hosting the discussion!
