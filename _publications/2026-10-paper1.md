excerpt: 'In this paper, our multiwavelength study of NGC 4936 reveals nearly 200 million solar masses of molecular gas in a weak X-ray cool core, alongside ionised gas and dust filaments extending roughly 15 kpc. No atomic hydrogen (H I) is detected in NGC 4936, yet MeerKAT observations uncover 20 H I structures in the surrounding environment, including ten without optical counterparts. We also find evidence of nuclear X-ray and millimetre variability on timescales of days, indicating a low-luminosity active galactic nucleus. The results favour atmospheric cooling as the primary origin of the central molecular gas, with galaxy interactions potentially promoting condensation and shaping the multiphase medium.'
