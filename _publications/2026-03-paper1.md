title: "HST view of NGC 5044: Constraints on Filament Widths, Magnetic Support, Multiphase Structure, and Comparison with Cluster Environments"
collection: publications
permalink: /publication/2026-03-paper1
excerpt: 'We used high-resolution imaging from the Hubble Space Telescope to study ionized gas filaments in the brightest group galaxy NGC 5044. The widths of these filaments scale with ambient pressure, directly linking small-scale filament properties to large-scale intragroup conditions. By combining HST data with ALMA and MUSE observations, we constrained gas column densities and magnetic field strengths, showing that magnetic support, turbulence, and cosmic rays help stabilize the filaments against collapse. NGC 5044 shows no ongoing star formation. We predict that lower-pressure group environments produce broader, more diffuse filaments with weaker molecular gas alignment. This work connects AGN feedback, magnetic fields, and dust survival to the origin and stability of multiphase gas in galaxies and galaxy clusters.'
