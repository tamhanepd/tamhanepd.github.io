title: "Constraining Star Formation in M87 Using Deep Hubble Space Telescope UV Data"
collection: publications
permalink: /publication/2025-08-paper1
excerpt: 'We analyzed one of the deepest HST UV data in the F275W filter of M87 and provide the most accurate estimate of its low-level star formation rate and history. We found that the star formation rate in M87 is extremely low at ~$2 \times 10^{-5} \, M_\odot \, {\rm yr}^{-1}$ with a burst-like star formation history, with a primary burst around 125 Myr ago that formed approximately 1000 $M_\odot$ stars. This bust is not associated with the current and previous AGN outburst and likely corresponds to an even earlier episode of AGN activity. The last two outbursts have not triggered any star formation. We also did not detect star formation related to the UV-optical filaments detected in M87 indicating that the star formation in M87 has been suppressed for ~200 Myr. We also detected a hotspot created by the counterjet of M87 in the UV images and constrain its spectral index.

The data from this work will be publicly released at: [https://archive.stsci.edu/hlsp/m87-hst-uv/](https://archive.stsci.edu/hlsp/m87-hst-uv/)'
