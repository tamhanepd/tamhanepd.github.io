title: "JWST reveals how black holes are fed: kiloparsec-scale multiphase filaments feed sub-kiloparsec circumnuclear disks"
collection: publications
permalink: /publication/2026-06-paper1
excerpt: "In this work, we analyzed JWST NIRSpec data of the central region of NGC 4696, brightest central galaxy of the Cenaturus galaxy cluster. The data revealed a swirl of warm molecular gas co-spatial with the 'S-shaped' feature previously reported in HST imaging, connecting to a circumnuclear rotating disk ~100 pc in radius. The disk is rotating with velocities of +/-200 km/s. This result directly connects kiloparsec scale cooling filaments feeding the circumnuclear disk within the Bondi radius of the central supermassive black hole closing the AGN feedback loop."
