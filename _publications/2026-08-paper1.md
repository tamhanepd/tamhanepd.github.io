title: "Mapping gas accretion and stellar kinematics to sub-kiloparsec scales in NGC 4696 with JWST/NIRSpec"
collection: publications
permalink: /publication/2026-08-paper1
excerpt: "Using JWST/NIRSpec IFU observations, we resolve the inner 600 pc of NGC 4696, revealing a compact, rotating circumnuclear disk around its central supermassive black hole. The gas kinematics indicate a black hole mass of $\sim 10^9$ M$_\odot$, a resolved sphere of influence, and an accretion rate of $\sim 18$ M$_\odot$ yr$^{-1}$. The JWST data uncover strong velocity gradients and complex, high-dispersion gas components that are unresolved in previous optical, molecular, and X-ray observations."
