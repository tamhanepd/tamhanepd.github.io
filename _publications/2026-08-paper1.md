excerpt: 'Using JWST/NIRSpec IFU observations, we resolve the inner 600 pc of NGC 4696, revealing a compact, rotating circumnuclear disk around its central supermassive black hole. The gas kinematics indicate a black hole mass of ~$10^9$ solar masses, a resolved sphere of influence, and an accretion rate of ~ 18solar masses per year. The JWST data uncover strong velocity gradients and complex, high-dispersion gas components that are unresolved in previous optical, molecular, and X-ray observations.'
